## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects



*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟



- **[ERPNext FSM](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/frappe/erpnext?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/stargazers)  

  **100% open-source Field Service Management app for ERPNext**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **The leading open-source FSM solution** — **designed to manage end-to-end field service operations** . **Scheduling, dispatch, work orders, and customer management** integrated with ERPNext's accounting, purchasing, and inventory modules . **Best when service work is tied tightly to ERP and you can configure business workflows** . **Limitation: dispatch usability requires configuration and compromises compared to dedicated FSM apps** . 🏗️



- **[BlazeResolver](https://github.com/DikshantJangra/BlazeResolver)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/DikshantJangra/BlazeResolver?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/DikshantJangra/BlazeResolver/stargazers)  

  **Open-source AI harness for customer service**, MIT licensed. **Policy-gated action with 4-adapter architecture** — OrderSource, RefundGateway, TicketSink, MenuControl . **Correlate Engine** clusters complaints by {category, dishId, branchId} and emits systemic incidents instead of N tickets . **Money-Gate HITL**: auto-resolves claims ≤ ₹300, gates larger amounts to supervisor queue . **The most advanced open-source field service AI harness** . 🎯



- **[openMAINT](https://github.com/openMAINT/openMAINT)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/openMAINT/openMAINT?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/openMAINT/openMAINT/stargazers)  

  **Open-source CMMS and facility maintenance software**, open-source. **Best fit when "field" means buildings, facilities, plants, campuses, and assets** . **Work orders belong to assets, buildings, or equipment** . **Maintenance history, preventive schedules, and technical records** . **Not for contractor FSM dispatching to outside customers** . 🏢



- **[openCRX](https://github.com/openCRX/openCRX)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/openCRX/openCRX?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/openCRX/openCRX/stargazers)  

  **Open-source CRM with service and workflow capabilities**, open-source. **Adaptable for customer records, service cases, activities, and issue tracking** . **Relevant for technical teams whose field-service workflow is customer/case driven rather than route-dispatch driven** . **Will not feel like a modern mobile-first FSM product** . 🏛️



- **[OpenBoxes](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/openboxes/openboxes?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes/stargazers)  

  **Open-source inventory and supply-chain software**, open-source. **Useful for stockrooms, warehouses, replenishment, lot tracking, and parts movement** . **Parts are often the hidden bottleneck in field service** — a dispatcher can schedule the right technician but the job fails if the part is missing . **Use as inventory layer or parts-management companion, not entire FSM stack** . 📦



- **[LibreWO](https://github.com/LibreWO/LibreWO)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/LibreWO/LibreWO?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/LibreWO/LibreWO/stargazers)  

  **Lightweight work-order and customer-management project**, open-source. **Aimed at small computer repair and service shops** . **Much narrower than other tools** — for small repair shops, a simple customer and work-order app may be more useful than a broad ERP . **Not enough for multi-crew field service** . 🔧



- **[Fleetbase](https://github.com/fleetbase/fleetbase)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/fleetbase/fleetbase?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/fleetbase/fleetbase/stargazers)  

  **Open-source modular logistics platform**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **2,562 GitHub stars** — **comprehensive open-source logistics suite with API, TMS, driver and customer apps** . **Open Source Alternative to** traditional fleet management platforms . **Streamlined operations and development** . 🚚



- **[Traccar](https://github.com/traccar/traccar)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/traccar/traccar?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/traccar/traccar/stargazers)  

  **Open-source GPS tracking server**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **One of three primary open-source fleet management systems evaluated in academic research** . **200+ GPS protocols supported**. **Real-time tracking, geofencing, and event alerts** . **The de facto standard for open-source fleet tracking** . 🗺️



- **[OpenGTS](https://github.com/OpenGTS/OpenGTS)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/OpenGTS/OpenGTS?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/OpenGTS/OpenGTS/stargazers)  

  **Open GPS Tracking System**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **One of the three primary open-source fleet management systems** evaluated in academic research alongside Traccar and OpenERP . **Java-based, supports multiple GPS devices**. **Web-based fleet tracking with reports and alerts** . 📍



- **[Budibase](https://github.com/Budibase/budibase)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Budibase/budibase?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Budibase/budibase/stargazers)  

  **Open-source low-code platform for building internal apps**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **Can be used to build job intake forms, inspection workflows, asset update screens, approval queues, and technician status updates** . **Good fit when existing process is custom enough that no packaged FSM product fits cleanly** . **Tradeoff: you become the product team** — scheduling rules, notifications, offline behavior, permissions, and reporting need to be designed . 🛠️



- **[Tauri FSM Desktop](https://github.com/topics/field-service-management)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/...?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/.../stargazers)  

  **Desktop field service management app**, open-source. **Job board with wizard, draft auto-save, proof attachments, dispute/resolve workflow, and overdue detection** . **Built with Tauri v2, SvelteKit 5, and SQLite** . 🖥️



- **[SAP FSM AI Scheduling](https://github.com/topics/field-service-management)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/...?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/.../stargazers)  

  **SAP Field Service Management AI scheduling reference implementation**, open-source. **Reference implementation and tools for artificial intelligence scheduling** . **SAP-backed open-source FSM AI** . 🤖
